# A Coarse Study of Effect of Music on Mental Health
This Study follows scientific approach in analyzing the data between music and its effect on mental health.

Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/catherinerasgaitis/mxmh-survey-results/data

**Problem and Objective**: Mental health issue is escalating in recent years. This Paper aims at looking for data to show music as a remediation to mental problem.

**Hypothesis**: There is relation between music and mental health.

**Research and Scope**: The study looks for recent research on music effect on mental health. Mental health is a general term while this research takes a deeper look at different types of mental disorder, including but not limited depression and anxiety as the most well-known illness. Secondly, analysis on music focuses not only on music listening, but also engagement like instruments and composition.

**EDA and Data Cleansing**: An exploratory data analysis (EDA) process is adopted to screen through the dataset applied in this Study. By EDA, the scope is defined with data to be focused. Irrelevant, erroneous and non-sense data and null value will be excluded and cleaned.

**Analysis and Implication**: The data is analyzed in univariate, bivariate and multivariate angles. Observation and implication are inspected upon the relation found in the data.

**Limitation and Constraint**: The Paper examines the limitation of the Study and constraint of the data, of which respective possible improvement is proposed. A conclusion is drawn with a prospect on future study.
